Masa Madre · Buenos Aires

Dear friend,

Are you looking for a little more room?

Room to follow the questions that keep tugging at you—and people who meet your curiosity with some of their own?

I’m inviting 20 curious, self-directed people to spend time together in Buenos Aires, sharing everyday life, meals, and ideas across different disciplines. There’s no assigned theme and no pitch to prepare. We’ll make time to wander, find one another, and make something tangible together.

Masa Madre is my first experiment toward a pop-up city: a temporary community shaped by the people who join it. I’d love for you to be part of it.

With warmth,Cecilia

A first version of a bigger idea

First, get curious. Then, make.

This gathering is an early version of a longer pop-up-city idea. We’ll leave room for people to discover one another before choosing what to work on.

Arrive with curiosity

Meet, wander, notice.

Share meals and questions. Show one another what you care about. Follow a conversation, join a walk, invite people into a practice, or take quiet time. No one assigns the group a problem to solve.

Follow a thread

Make something real.

Give time to a question, experiment, or piece of work that feels alive to you. Work on your own or with others, and let the people and ideas in the room shape what comes next.

Who might feel at home

People who follow their curiosity into action, enjoy the company of different kinds of minds, and want to help make a warm community—not just attend one. You don’t need a finished project or a particular title. Bring an open question, some generosity, and a willingness to make something real.
